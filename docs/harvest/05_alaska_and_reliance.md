# Addendum: Alaska North Slope thesis and Reliance round, master v7 and v8 (4 Nov 2026)

## Alaska (Anyanwu 2007, UAF MS thesis, read end to end)
- 28 stock tank (dead) oils from ConocoPhillips Alaska fields, sample numbers 1 to 28 (no 15). Tables used: 4.1 CPM WAT, 4.2 viscometry WAT, 4.3 CPM WAT and WDT, 4.4 comparison, 4.5 thermal history, 5.2 SG and API, 5.3 pour points, A1 density, B1 to B7 viscosity.
- 37 rows (ANS01 to ANS37, 20 oils): 14 CPM, 20 viscometry (history erased), 3 viscometry with history kept (samples 4, 5, 6). Tier B-. WAT and WDT values agree with a second read.
- Wax content was NOT measured in the thesis (listed as future work), so these rows stay out of models that need wax.
- Pour point: 8 measured (reported = recorded + 3 C, Lawler 334, D97 style); 19 samples recorded below -31 C (test limit), entered as -28 C, flagged pour_censored.
- 7 samples have no WAT (11, 12, 18 to 22): data/bulk/alaska_no_wat_samples.csv.
- The OSTI abstract quotes wax up to 28.57 wt%: not in the thesis; may be in the DOE report (OSTI 963363, paywalled).
