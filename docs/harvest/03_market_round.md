# Addendum: market-world round and master v5 (4 Nov 2026)

## What was searched
Six agents looked outside academia for commercial crude quality documents: regulatory filings (33 sources), environmental impact assessments (22), company assay libraries (34), tenders and lab certificates (39), benchmark and price data (43), trade press (28).

## Finding
- Prices, trade flows and benchmark specifications carry no WAT. No document found ties a price penalty to WAT.
- Company assay libraries (Total, Exxon, Tullow, Equinor and others) carry no whole-crude WAT.
- The only real yield: Reliance KG-D6 condensate tender assays (Bureau Veritas Dubai, ASTM D8420 / IP 389A, UOP 46, D97, D445).
